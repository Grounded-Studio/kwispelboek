FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Wagbook free?

Yes. You can keep up to 2 pets for free with all logs, reminders, the weight chart, the travel checklist, the missing poster and backups. PRO adds unlimited pets and photos, the PDF overviews and AI scanning.

Do I need an account?

No. Wagbook works without an account and stores everything on your own phone. We have no server that receives your data.

What happens when I get a new phone?

Make a backup in Settings: one file with all pets, records and photos. Keep it in Google Drive or email it to yourself, and restore it on your new phone.

How do the reminders work?

For each vaccination or treatment you choose how far in advance you want a reminder, plus one on the day itself at the time you set. Medication reminds you at every time of day, with ‘Given’ and ‘Later’ buttons.

Is it only for dogs?

No. Wagbook is for dogs, cats, rabbits, birds and other pets. Vaccination suggestions adapt to the species, and birds are weighed in grams.

What happens to my photo when I scan with AI?

Only when you choose to scan, that one photo is sent to Google (Gemini) to read the text. We do not store it. You always check the result before anything is saved.

Does Wagbook give medical advice?

No. Wagbook helps you keep track and remember. Suggested schedules are common defaults; always follow the advice of your own vet.

When is Wagbook available?

Wagbook is coming soon to Google Play for Android. In Dutch the app is called Kwispelboek. An iPhone version is not planned yet.
